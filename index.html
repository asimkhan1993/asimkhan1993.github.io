PROFILE

About Me

I am a PhD graduate in Control Science and Engineering from Shandong University, China (September 2026), with research interests spanning intelligent sensing, machine learning, deep learning and advanced signal processing.

My research investigates intelligent approaches for fault detection, diagnosis and health assessment of complex engineering systems, particularly rotating machinery operating under variable and non-stationary conditions.

I work on physics-inspired deep learning, frequency-aware feature extraction, time-frequency analysis, transformer-based architectures and intelligent computational frameworks for engineering applications.

My broader research interests also include digital twins, wireless sensor networks, intelligent optimisation and data-driven engineering solutions.

My research objective is to bridge the gap between physical understanding, signal intelligence and advanced artificial intelligence to develop reliable, interpretable and practical engineering solutions.
