About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer and PhD graduate in Control Science and Engineering from Shandong University, China, having completed my doctoral degree in September 2026.

My research focuses on intelligent fault detection, vibration signal analysis, advanced signal processing, and the integration of machine learning and deep learning for complex engineering applications. My work spans rotating machinery condition monitoring, fault diagnosis, health assessment, and intelligent sensing, alongside research involving wireless sensor networks and energy-aware optimization.

I am particularly interested in developing interpretable physics-inspired neural networks, frequency-aware learning architectures, and intelligent diagnostic methodologies for reliable engineering systems under non-stationary and variable operating conditions.

Research Philosophy: Bridging physical understanding, advanced signal processing, and artificial intelligence to develop reliable and interpretable solutions for real-world engineering challenges.

Research Projects & Contributions

Selected research directions reflecting my contributions to intelligent sensing and AI-driven engineering.

01. Physics-Inspired Deep Learning for Machinery Fault Diagnosis

Investigation of physics-inspired deep learning architectures, including dilated Kronecker convolution and intelligent feature learning, for robust fault diagnosis of rotating machinery under variable operating conditions.

02. Entropy-Augmented Multiscale Feature Fusion

Development of entropy-augmented multiscale feature fusion methodologies for extracting discriminative information from vibration signals and improving rolling bearing fault diagnosis.

03. Frequency-Aware Transformer-Based Fault Diagnosis

Investigation of frequency-aware transformer fusion techniques for intelligent feature representation and fault diagnosis of rolling bearings under variable operating conditions.

04. Energy-Aware Optimization in Wireless Sensor Networks

Research on learning-based mobile charging, energy-aware resource allocation and optimization of rechargeable sensor networks under stochastic energy dynamics.

05. Battery Swapping Infrastructure Optimization

Research involving multi-objective optimization, battery degradation considerations, geographical information systems and renewable-powered battery swapping infrastructure.
