About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer with PhD in Control Science and Engineering from Shandong University, China. My research focuses on intelligent fault detection using signal analysis, vibration signal processing, signal processing in wireless networks for path planning, integration of machine learning and deep learning for complex engineering and research solution, etc.

My research aims to develop interpretable physics-inspired neural networks, intelligent approaches for reliable health monitoring under non-stationary operating conditions, digital twins, wireless networks etc.
