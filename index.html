About Me

I am a PhD graduate in Control Science and Engineering from Shandong University, China (September 2026). My research focuses on intelligent sensing, vibration signal processing, machine learning and deep learning for condition monitoring, fault diagnosis and health assessment of rotating machinery.

My research aims to develop physics-informed, interpretable and intelligent approaches for reliable machinery health monitoring under complex operating conditions.
